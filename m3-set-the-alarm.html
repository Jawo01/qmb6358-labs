Set the Alarm

You manage the book's 12-ounce bottling line, where every day a test decides whether to stop the line. Please pick a significance level and run a simulated year to find the setting that costs the least.

This lab takes about 20 minutes and also works on a phone. Nothing is graded or saved.

1Two ways to be wrong

Each morning, the plant samples 10 bottles and runs a one-sided t-test to check whether the average fill is above 12 ounces. If the evidence is strong enough, the line is stopped for recalibration §4.10.3 · p. 181. Of course, the test can be wrong in two ways §4.5.1 · p. 153:

MistakeWhat happensCost in the book's plant
False stop
Type I error		The line was fine, but the test stopped it.$50,000 per stop
Missed overfill
Type II error		The line was overfilling, but the test let it run.$12,000 per day, until caught

The significance level (α) is the false-alarm rate that you are willing to tolerate on days when the line is fine §4.5.3 · p. 154. That is, a lower α means fewer false stops, but also more missed overfill.

4Peeking: looking at the data until you find something

This step looks at a different trap from the same chapter §4.9.4 · p. 174. An email A/B test runs for 14 days with 1,000 recipients per version per day. In this case the two versions are identical (both convert at 3.0%), so any "winner" is a false alarm.

Policy A looks only once (on day 14), while Policy B looks every day and declares a winner on the first day that p < 0.05.

One test: the p-value each day

False winners across tests

The book's remedy is good governance, i.e. state the hypotheses and the threshold before the analysis, disclose how many tests (or looks) were run, and confirm exploratory wins with follow-up data §4.9.4 · p. 174.

Then check the answer

  • Does it recommend a lower α when false stops get more expensive, and a higher α when overfill gets more expensive? Please change the costs and ask again.

  • How close is its recommendation to the cheapest α in step 3? If it differs, which assumptions explain the gap?
