QMB 6358 · Data Analytics for Business

Decision Labs

These short interactive exercises are meant as a supplement to the readings in Business Analytics in the Age of AI (Jank, 2026) and The Math Behind the Machines (Novikov, 2025). Please work through each lab after you have completed the reading for that module or project; your Reading Guide (or the project page) links to it. All you need to do is click buttons and move sliders. Nothing is graded or saved, and the labs also work on a phone.

Module 1

Week 1 · Chapter 1

From Question to Decision

In this lab you improve one vague request to an AI tool step by step, compare the answers that come back, and decide which change made the biggest difference.

The Mix Trap

In this lab, one region appears to be the weakest performer. Please follow the chapter's rule for comparing groups and find out what the comparison is really telling you.

The Uncertain Zone

In this lab you run the book's market-share panel 100 times. You will see what “95% confident” really means, and what to do when an interval straddles the break-even point.

About 15 minutesWeek 3 · Chapter 4

Set the Alarm

In this lab you run the book's bottling line for a simulated year, choose the significance level, and find the setting with the lowest cost.

Bending the Line

In this lab you find out where a straight line bends in the book's pricing data, switch to a log-log model, and then work out in which product category a price increase would actually raise revenue.

Forecast Ranges

In this lab you build the lecture's Coca-Cola forecast one piece at a time, test it on a year that it has not seen, and then plan for a range of outcomes instead of a single number.

Kaggle Project

With the Kaggle competition

Too Good to Be True

In this lab you add predictors until the fit looks excellent and then see what happens on the leaderboard, including a feature that is too good to be true. At the end, you review the decisions that an AI tool made for you.

Visualization Project

With the Tableau dashboard

Same Data, Different Story

In this lab the data never change, only the chart choices do (e.g. where the axis starts or how wide the bins are). Please watch what a viewer would conclude at each step.
