Fair Comparisons

At first glance, Hybrid sales reps seem to sell far more than everyone else. Please build the regression yourself, one driver at a time, and watch what happens to that advantage once you compare like with like.

This lab takes about 20 minutes and also works on a phone. Nothing is graded or saved.
