The Mix Trap

In this lab, one of the regions looks like the weakest performer. Before anyone acts on that, please follow the chapter's rule for comparing groups and find out what the comparison is really telling you.

This lab takes about 15 minutes and also works on a phone. Nothing is graded or saved.

1The South looks weakest

We start with 1,000 transactions from the past year, set up in the style of the book's sales case (i.e. five regions and four product categories). The chart shows each region's median transaction revenue. Since revenue is right-skewed, a few very large orders would pull the mean up; that is why the median is the better summary of a typical sale §2.5.4 · p. 50.

2Counts first: exposure and mix

The chapter warns that a comparison of groups can hide two things: the exposure (i.e. how many observations each group contributes) and the mix (i.e. what the group is selling) §2.6.1 · p. 53.
